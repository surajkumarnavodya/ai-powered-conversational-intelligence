Bundled font licenses
=====================

This application self-hosts the typefaces offered by the font picker in
Settings > Appearance (see frontend/src/lib/fonts.ts). They are bundled as
WOFF2 and served from this application's own origin; no font is fetched
from a third-party CDN.

Every bundled typeface is licensed under the SIL Open Font License v1.1
(OFL-1.1), which permits bundling and redistribution provided the copyright
notice and license accompany the font files. That notice is reproduced
below. The full, unmodified license text for each family ships inside its
npm package at node_modules/@fontsource-variable/<family>/LICENSE.

Full license text: https://openfontlicense.org/

The "System default" and "System monospace" options bundle nothing -- they
resolve to whichever sans-serif or monospace face the operating system
already provides, and carry no license obligation here.
