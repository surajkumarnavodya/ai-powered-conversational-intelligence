#   * CVE-2026-31900 / PYSEC-2026-2120 -- supply-chain issue in the Action.

#     letting `tool.black.required-version` point at a malicious repo.
#     Patched in 26.10.1. Ignored in CI with that reasoning rather than
#     taken, because taking it is not free: see below.
